# Code of conduct

This is a small project. The rules are short.

## Expected

Be decent to people. Assume the person on the other side is trying to help. Critique code,
not the person who wrote it. When you disagree, say what you would do instead and why.

## Not accepted

Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language of any kind. Publishing anyone's
private information. Sustained disruption of issues or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening an issue, or privately through
[a security advisory](https://github.com/zopeVaibhav/ezyshare/security/advisories/new) if
it should not be public. Reports are handled by the maintainer, who may edit, lock, or
remove content and block accounts.

This is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
kept short deliberately.
